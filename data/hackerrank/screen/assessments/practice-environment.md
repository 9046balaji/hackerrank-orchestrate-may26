# Practice Environment and Apply Tab

## Finding the Apply Tab
The Apply tab appears in the top navigation bar of your HackerRank profile. If you cannot see it:
1. Make sure you are logged in to your account.
2. Clear your browser cache and refresh the page.
3. Try a different browser or disable browser extensions.

## Practice Submissions
If your practice submissions are not working:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Try a different programming language or problem.
3. Contact support if the issue persists across multiple problems and browsers.

# Lost or Stolen Visa Traveller's Cheques

If your Visa Traveller's Cheques have been lost or stolen, contact the issuer immediately.

## Citicorp Traveller's Cheques
- **Freephone (US):** 1-800-645-6556
- **Collect (international):** 1-813-623-1709
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday 6:30 am–2:30 pm EST
- **Automated service:** Available 24/7 in English and Spanish
- Outside business hours: your call is recorded and returned the next business day.

## What to have ready
- Cheque serial numbers
- Where and when you bought the cheques
- How and when they were lost or stolen
- Issuer name (e.g., Citicorp)

## Refund
Refunds can typically be arranged within 24 hours, subject to terms and conditions.

## Additional steps
- Notify local police in the location where cheques were lost/stolen.
- If you cannot reach Citicorp, use Visa's traveller's-cheque contact form on visa.co.in.

# Interview Inactivity Timers

## Default inactivity times
HackerRank interviews have separate inactivity timers for candidates and interviewers. If a user is inactive (no mouse movement, keystrokes, or tab activity) for the configured period, they may be moved to the waiting room or lobby.

## Candidate inactivity
Candidates are typically given a shorter inactivity window. Screen sharing activity (by the candidate) counts as active participation.

## Interviewer inactivity
Interviewers watching a screen share may not appear active to the system if they are not interacting with the HackerRank tab. This is a known scenario — interviewers should periodically interact with the HackerRank interface to avoid being marked inactive.

## Requesting extended inactivity times
If the default inactivity times are too short for your interview format, you can request an extension by contacting HackerRank support with your account details and use case. Enterprise accounts may have configurable inactivity settings.

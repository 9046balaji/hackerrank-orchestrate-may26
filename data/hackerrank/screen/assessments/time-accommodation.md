# Adding Extra Time for Candidates

You can add time accommodations to HackerRank tests before or after invitations are sent.

## Steps to add time accommodation after invite
1. Log in to your HackerRank for Work account.
2. Go to the **Tests** tab.
3. Select the test you want to modify.
4. Go to the **Candidates** tab.
5. Select the checkbox next to the candidate(s) you want to accommodate.
6. Click **More > Add Time Accommodation**.
7. Enter the accommodation percentage in multiples of five.
8. Click **Save**. A success message appears.

## Notes
- Time accommodation can also be added before the invite has been sent.
- The percentage is applied to the base test duration.
- For detailed steps, refer to: https://support.hackerrank.com/articles/4811403281-adding-extra-time-for-candidates

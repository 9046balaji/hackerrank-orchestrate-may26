# HackerRank Certificates

## Name incorrect on certificate
If your name appears incorrectly on your HackerRank certificate, this requires verification by our support team to ensure the change is legitimate. Please contact support with:
- Proof of identity (government-issued ID)
- Your HackerRank account email
- The correct name that should appear

## Accessing your certificate
1. Log in to your HackerRank account.
2. Go to your profile and click **Certificates**.
3. Download or share the certificate from the certificate detail page.

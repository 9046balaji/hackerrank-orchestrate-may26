# Lost or Stolen Visa Card

## From India
Call Visa India at **000-800-100-1219** to report a lost or stolen card.

## From anywhere in the world (24/7)
Visa's Global Customer Assistance Service: **+1 303 967 1090**
- Available 24/7
- Can block your card within approximately 30 minutes of being reported
- Can arrange emergency cash disbursement
- Can arrange a replacement card

## What to do immediately
1. Call the number above to report the card lost or stolen.
2. Request an emergency card block.
3. Ask about emergency cash disbursement if you need immediate funds.
4. Request a replacement card.

## If you are abroad
If you are traveling internationally, Visa's Global Customer Assistance Service (+1 303 967 1090) can assist you regardless of your location.

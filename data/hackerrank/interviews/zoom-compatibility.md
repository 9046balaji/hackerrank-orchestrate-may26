# Zoom Compatibility in HackerRank

## If Zoom connectivity fails in the compatibility check
1. Ensure Zoom desktop app is installed and up to date.
2. Check that your browser has permission to access your camera and microphone.
3. Disable VPN or firewall rules that may block Zoom's UDP ports.
4. Try switching to a different network (e.g., mobile hotspot instead of corporate Wi-Fi).
5. Ensure no other applications are using your camera or microphone.
6. Restart your browser and re-run the compatibility check.

## System requirements
- Stable internet connection (minimum 1 Mbps upload/download recommended).
- Chrome 70+ or Firefox 68+ recommended.
- Camera and microphone access enabled in browser settings.

## Still not working?
If you have tried all the above steps and the Zoom connectivity check still fails, contact HackerRank support and provide your system specifications and a screenshot of the error.

# How to Dispute a Visa Charge

## Steps to dispute a charge
1. First, contact the merchant directly and request a resolution or refund. Keep records of all communication.
2. If the merchant does not resolve the issue, contact your card-issuing bank to initiate a formal dispute.
3. Your bank (not Visa directly) handles disputes on your behalf.

## What information to have ready
- The transaction date and amount
- The merchant name
- A description of what went wrong (wrong item, not received, unauthorized, etc.)
- Any communication records with the merchant

## Timeline
- Disputes are typically investigated within 30–60 days.
- You may receive a provisional credit while the investigation is ongoing.

## Contact
Contact your card issuer (the bank that issued your Visa card) to start the dispute process. For general Visa support, visit visa.co.in/support.

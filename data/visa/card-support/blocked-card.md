# Blocked Visa Card While Traveling

If your Visa card has been blocked while you are abroad, follow these steps:

## Immediate steps
1. Contact your card-issuing bank immediately — the number is typically on the back of your card.
2. Explain that you are traveling and your card was blocked unexpectedly.
3. Your bank can verify your identity and unblock the card remotely in most cases.

## If you cannot reach your bank
Call Visa's Global Customer Assistance Service: **+1 303 967 1090** (available 24/7)

## Why cards get blocked while traveling
- Unusual spending patterns detected by fraud monitoring
- Transactions from unfamiliar countries flagged automatically
- The card may have reached its transaction limit

## Prevention
Before traveling, notify your bank of your travel plans to prevent automatic blocks.

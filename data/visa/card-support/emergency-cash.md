# Visa Emergency Cash Disbursement

If you are in an emergency and need cash, Visa's Emergency Cash Disbursement service can help.

## How to access emergency cash
1. Call Visa's Global Customer Assistance Service: **+1 303 967 1090** (available 24/7)
2. Explain your situation and request Emergency Cash Disbursement.
3. Provide your card details and current location.
4. Visa will arrange for funds to be available through a local bank or financial institution.

## Eligibility
- This service is available to cardholders traveling away from home.
- The disbursed amount is charged to your card account.
- Fees may apply depending on your card issuer.

## What to have ready
- Your Visa card number (or the last 4 digits if your card is lost)
- Your current location
- Your cardholder verification information

## Notes
- Emergency cash is typically available within hours.
- You do not need your physical card to use this service.
- Contact your issuing bank for information on fees and limits.

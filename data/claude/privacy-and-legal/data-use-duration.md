# Data Use for Model Improvement

## If you have opted in
If you have opted in to allow Anthropic to use your conversation data for model training:
- Your data may be used to improve Claude models as described in Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
- You can withdraw consent at any time by changing your privacy settings.

## Managing your data preferences
1. Log in to claude.ai.
2. Go to **Settings > Privacy**.
3. Review and update your data usage preferences.

## Opting out
You can opt out of data use for model training at any time. After opting out, your future conversations will not be used for training. Historical data use is subject to Anthropic's data retention policies.

For more details, see Anthropic's Privacy Policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

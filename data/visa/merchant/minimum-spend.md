# Merchant Minimum Spend Requirements

## General rule
Visa's standard rules do not allow merchants to impose minimum purchase amounts for Visa credit card transactions. However, there are exceptions.

## US territories exception (Dodd-Frank Act)
Under the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act in the United States, merchants **may** require a minimum purchase of up to **$10** for credit card transactions, including in US territories such as:
- US Virgin Islands
- Puerto Rico
- Guam
- American Samoa

This is a legal exception, not a Visa policy violation.

## What you can do
If you believe a merchant is incorrectly requiring a minimum spend (outside of the legally permitted exceptions), you may:
1. Ask the merchant to clarify their policy.
2. Report the issue to your card issuer.
3. Submit a complaint via visa.co.in/support.

## Debit cards
Different rules may apply to Visa debit (check) cards regarding minimum transactions.

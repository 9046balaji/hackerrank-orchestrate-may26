# Subscription Management

## Pausing or cancelling your subscription
Subscription changes (pausing, cancelling, or modifying your plan) must be handled by the HackerRank account management team. Please contact your Customer Success Manager or submit a support request with your account details.

## Refund policy
Refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the billing team. Contact support with your payment details and reason for the refund request.

## Upgrading your plan
To upgrade your HackerRank subscription, contact your Customer Success Manager or reach out to sales@hackerrank.com.

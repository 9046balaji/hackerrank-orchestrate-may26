# Identity Theft Guidance

If you believe your identity has been stolen, take these steps immediately:

## Immediate actions
1. **Contact your bank and card issuers** — Report all cards and accounts that may be compromised.
2. **Request card cancellation** — Cancel all cards and request replacements.
3. **File a police report** — Report the identity theft to your local police. Get a case number.
4. **Alert credit bureaus** — Place a fraud alert or freeze on your credit report.

## Visa-specific steps
- Call Visa's Global Customer Assistance Service: **+1 303 967 1090** (24/7)
- Your card issuer's fraud department can review recent transactions for unauthorized activity.

## Document everything
Keep records of every communication, case numbers, and any unauthorized transactions.

**Note:** Identity theft is a serious crime. Engage the appropriate authorities (police, consumer protection agencies) in addition to contacting your financial institutions.

# Deleting a HackerRank Account (Google Login)

If your account was created via Google login, you do not have a HackerRank password by default. You must set one before deleting your account.

## Steps
1. Go to the HackerRank login page and click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter the email linked to your Google login and follow the instructions to set a new password.
3. Log in using the new password.
4. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
5. Scroll to the **Delete Account** section.
6. Click **Delete Account** and follow the prompts, entering your new password to confirm.

**Warning:** Deleting your account will permanently remove all data and cannot be undone.
